Reflux Guide

Nighttime Flare-Up Checklist

  1. Sit Upright Immediately: Let gravity pull acid down from your esophagus.
  2. Loosen Waistbands: Remove any abdominal pressure.
  3. Sip Lukewarm Water: Small sips wash pepsin and acid downward. Do not chug cold water.
  4. Take an Antacid/Alginate: Use Tums (calcium carbonate) or Gaviscon for rapid neutralization.
  5. Sleep on Your Left Side: Keeps the stomach-esophageal junction above the pool of gastric acid.
